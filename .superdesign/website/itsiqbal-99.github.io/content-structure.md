# Ma'had An-Najah Putri Batam

## Business Context
- **Type/Industry:** Islamic boarding school (pesantren) for girls; SMP & SMA (grades 7–12).
- **What they do:** Full-time education combining Qur'an memorization (tahfidz), Islamic studies (diniyah), formal secular subjects, and daily character formation (adab) in a residential setting.
- **Target audience:** Parents and prospective students seeking Qur'an-centered Islamic education for daughters; families in Indonesia.
- **Page goal:** Present institution's values, curriculum structure, facilities, and daily life; drive inquiry via "Hubungi Kami" (Contact Us).

# Page Layout & Structure

### Hero
Full-width dark navy background with photograph of veiled students (1.78:1) positioned right-bleeding off edge. Left third: stacked headline with tight letterspacing — h1 "Teguh di atas Sunnah." in white, then "Mulia dalam adab." in tan/gold — followed by 4-line body paragraph in light blue. Small tan label "MA'HAD SUNNAH KHUSUS PUTRI" sits above headline. Two CTAs below text: tan pill button "Mengenal Ma'had" (primary) + navy outlined pill "Video Profil" with play icon (secondary). Header navigation (sticky, relative position) sits above: left-aligned logo + institutional name, center horizontal nav 9 links (Tentang through FAQ), right: dark mode toggle + tan "Hubungi Kami" CTA button.

### Header / Navigation
Sticky horizontal bar, navy background with light-blue text for links. Left: white-background logo tile (rounded) + institution name in navy + tan tagline. Center: 9 nav links (Tentang, Visi & Misi, Program, Pendidikan, Fasilitas, Galeri, Karya Santriwati, FAQ) in navy. Right: circular dark-mode toggle + tan rounded-pill CTA "Hubungi Kami" with arrow icon. All nav links and CTA use navy or tan accent on hover.

### Primary CTA / Input
Two-tier: Hero contains tan pill button "Mengenal Ma'had" (links #tentang) + navy outlined "Video Profil" button. Secondary CTA "Hubungi Kami" (tan pill, top-right header) is persistent. Tan (gold) serves as primary accent; navy as secondary container/outline.

### Keunggulan Utama (Key Advantages)
- **Purpose:** Introduce four core differentiators at a glance.
- **Layout:** 2-column bento grid (light-blue background), 4 equal cells in 2×2. Each cell: navy h3 + body text in muted blue. Icons (shield, book, person, layers) in tan. Left edge: tan vertical rule behind "Keunggulan Utama" label.

### Ilmu sebagai bekal. Sunnah sebagai tuntunan. (Knowledge & Guidance)
- **Purpose:** Articulate the institution's pedagogical philosophy with three supporting pillars.
- **Layout:** Light-blue background section. H2 heading + body intro, then 3 cards in horizontal row. Each card: navy h3 + muted-blue body text. Icons in tan. Card backgrounds light blue with rounded corners.

### Arah Pendidikan (Educational Direction: Vision & Mission)
- **Purpose:** State vision, mission (5-point list), and curriculum pillars in one cohesive narrative.
- **Layout:** Numbered sections (01 Visi, 02 Misi) with navy headings, muted-blue body. Mission list uses ordered bullets. Below: "Program Pondok" section with 5 numbered items (Tahfidzul Qur'an, Tahfidzul Hadits, Bahasa Arab Dasar, Adab & Akhlaq, Mapel UN) in compact rows with tan numbers.

### Pelajaran agama dan sekolah, sesuai jenjang. (Curriculum by Grade Level)
- **Purpose:** Display subject offerings for SMP and SMA via tabbed interface.
- **Layout:** Light-blue background. Tab control (SMP | SMA) + nested tabs (Diniyah & Al-Qur'an | Pelajaran Umum | Pengembangan Diri). Active tab shows h3 + list of subjects (muted-blue text, 4–5 items per tab). Tab styling: navy text, tan underline on active.

### Fasilitas untuk belajar dan tinggal. (Facilities)
- **Purpose:** Showcase dormitory, classrooms, library, and sports/prayer areas via tabbed photo carousel.
- **Layout:** Light-blue background. Tablist: 4 tabs (Asrama putri | Kelas & laboratorium | Perpustakaan | Ibadah & olahraga), each showing photo count. Active tab displays large image (1.78:1) with rounded bottom corners, caption, and thumbnail strip below (3 small 1:1 thumbnails). Navigation arrows (left/right) + "01 / 03" counter below. Tabs styled: tan number + navy label.

### Melihat suasana pondok dari dekat. (Video Profile)
- **Purpose:** Embed institution's cinematic video introduction.
- **Layout:** Full-width dark navy overlay section. Left: h2 + label "VIDEO PROFIL" in tan. Right: large video thumbnail (1.78:1) with centered play button icon and "Lihat profil" label overlay.

### Belajar dan tumbuh bersama. (Gallery: Activities)
- **Purpose:** Display student life through curated photo collections (3 categories).
- **Layout:** Light-blue background. Tablist: 3 tabs (Halaqah Al-Qur'an | Ukhuwah & Kemandirian | Belajar di Setiap Ruang) with photo counts. Active tab: masonry grid, 2 columns, 4 cells (varying heights 1.78:1, 1:1). Lightbox interaction on tap/click. Navigation arrows + counter below grid.

### Ruang untuk berlatih, berkarya, dan bertumbuh. (Extracurriculars)
- **Purpose:** Introduce co-curricular offerings (skills development, clubs, projects).
- **Layout:** Light-blue background. Heading + intro. Tablist or card row (5–6 items) with navy h3 + muted-blue descriptions.

### Dari fajar hingga waktu istirahat. (Daily Schedule)
- **Purpose:** Walk through a typical day (4 blocks: Ibadah & Al-Qur'an | Pelajaran di Kelas | Keterampilan & Gerak | Belajar & Beristirahat).
- **Layout:** Uniform grid, 2 columns (light-blue background). Each cell: navy h3 + muted-blue description + icon in tan. Rounded corners on cells.

### Mengenal ma'had lebih dekat. (About / Detailed Info)
- **Purpose:** Deeper institutional background, values, and what sets them apart.
- **Layout:** 2-column layout. Left: navy h2 + 2–3 body paragraphs (muted blue). Right: feature image (1.33:1) or secondary narrative block.

### Kabar dari pondok. (News / Blog Feed)
- **Purpose:** Display recent updates, announcements, photos from campus life.
- **Layout:** Uniform grid, 2 columns. Each card: 1.78:1 image, navy h3 headline, muted-blue excerpt, "Baca selengkapnya" link in tan.

### Tetap terhubung dengan pondok. (Contact / Newsletter)
- **Purpose:** Facilitate inquiries, subscriptions, social links.
- **Layout:** Dark navy background. Left: navy h2 + body intro. Right: form inputs (email + button, or contact details). Button in tan. Social icons in tan/navy.

### Mari berkenalan lebih dekat. (FAQ / Get Started)
- **Purpose:** Answer common questions; lower friction for first-time visitors.
- **Layout:** Light-blue background. Left column: navy h2 "Mengenal ma'had lebih dekat" + 1–2 body paragraphs + tan "Tanyakan langsung" link. Right column: accordion (4–5 expandable questions, navy headings, muted-blue answers, tan expand/collapse icon).

### Footer
Navy background. Multi-column layout: institution name + logo (left), nav links (center columns), contact + social icons (right). All text light blue or tan (accent links). Copyright and legal links bottom.

---

**Notable patterns:** 
- **Accent color:** Tan/gold (buttons, icons, labels, active states); navy (headings, text, outlines).  
- **Background alternation:** Light blue + white sections alternate with dark navy sections throughout.  
- **Grid types:** Bento grids for key-advantages (2-col, 4 equal cells); masonry for gallery (2-col, varying heights); uniform for schedules & news (2-col, equal cells).  
- **Image side alternation:** Feature sections (curriculum, facilities, about) flip image placement left ↔ right across the page.  
- **Sticky elements:** Header navigation remains fixed at top; tab controls persist within their sections.  
- **Interactive depth:** Tabbed interfaces (curriculum, facilities, gallery, daily schedule) gate content; lightbox on gallery images; accordion on FAQ.  
- **Typography:** 2 font families; h1 uses tight letterspacing; h2/h3 in navy, body in muted blue or light blue; all corners rounded (buttons are full pills).
